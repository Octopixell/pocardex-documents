# Support Pocardex

Pocardex is built to help Pokémon TCG collectors manage and track their collections—without ads or paywalls. Development, updates, and new features take time and resources, and your support makes a real difference.

Pocardex is developed and maintained by Hyperdrive Labs. If you enjoy using Pocardex, consider supporting the project through Ko-fi, where you can:

- Send a one-time tip (like buying a coffee)
- Set up a small monthly subscription to support ongoing development

All contributions go directly to Hyperdrive Labs and are used to improve Pocardex: fixing bugs, adding new features, and keeping the app fast and reliable.

There’s no pressure—every bit of support is appreciated, whether it’s a single tip or an ongoing subscription. If you’d like to help, tap the button below to visit the Ko-fi page and choose what works best for you.

[Support Pocardex on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/hyperdrivelabs)

**_Note: The Ko-fi page is operated by Hyperdrive Labs, the company behind Pocardex._**

Thank you for being part of the Pocardex community.
